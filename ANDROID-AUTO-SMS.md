# Android vs iPhone SMS

A website cannot press your SIM. That is the same on both phones.

Android is closer to “direct send” because you can install an SMS Gateway app and grant SEND SMS. Then AnnaSetu talks to that app and texts go out without opening Messages.

iPhone cannot grant that permission to a student app.

SIH demo that always works: OTP is created in the database and shown after Send OTP. Judges accept this. Paid MSG91 is how government sites send automatic OTP.
