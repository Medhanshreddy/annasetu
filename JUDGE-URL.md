# Show app.annasetu.in instead of localhost

On the JUDGE laptop (Administrator Notepad):

1. Open
   C:\Windows\System32\drivers\etc\hosts
2. Add this line at the bottom:

   127.0.0.1    app.annasetu.in

3. Save.
4. Keep `node server.js` running.
5. Chrome:  http://app.annasetu.in:8080

That is still your laptop. It only hides the word localhost.
Real public https://annasetu.in needs a paid domain + hosting later.
